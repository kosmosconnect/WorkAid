How we work together

Three ways
to engage

Pick the commitment that suits the problem. All three start with the same paid or free first survey, depending on scope.

Model 01

One-off project

A defined scope with a fixed, itemised price — a terrace, a flat, a fit-out, a licence file. Closed on handover with warranty documents.

  • Fixed written scope and price
  • Milestone-linked payments
